# Hermes — Personal Google Workspace Integration

This is the information website for Nilesh's personal installation of **Hermes**, an AI assistant connected to Google Workspace through Google's APIs.

This installation is for the account owner's personal use. It is not a public sign-up service, and this website is not operated by Google or Nous Research.

## Purpose and requested access

With the account owner's authorization, the assistant can:

- Search and read Gmail, send messages, and manage message labels.
- Read and manage Google Calendar events.
- Access and manage Google Drive files.
- Read and edit Google Sheets spreadsheets and Google Docs documents.
- Read Google Contacts through the People API, when that API is enabled.

Actions depend on the Google permissions granted, the APIs enabled, and the owner's instructions. This site does not itself connect to Google or collect mailbox data.

## Privacy and control

Google authorization credentials are stored in the owner's local Hermes profile, not in this public website or its source repository. Workspace content retrieved by the assistant may be included in local conversation history and may be processed by the AI provider configured by the owner.

Read the [privacy policy](privacy.html) for access, processing, storage, sharing, and deletion details.
